Accessibility statement

This statement applies to the portfolio and booking website of Lucas Hale, independent barber. I want as many people as possible to be able to use it — and to book a cut without hassle.

Conformance status

This website aims to fully conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. It was designed and tested with accessibility in mind from the start.

What you can do on this site

  • Use every feature with a keyboard only: the menu, the cut explorer (arrow keys move between cuts), the cut finder quiz, the clipper guide slider, the portfolio filter, the photo viewer and the booking form. A “Skip to main content” link is the first thing you reach.
  • Use a screen reader such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack. Pages use proper headings, landmarks, labels and descriptive image text, and changes such as filter results are announced.
  • Choose Day shop (light) or After hours (dark) mode. The site follows your system setting by default, and the sun/moon button in the header switches it.
  • Read text at a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 in both themes and every accent colour. If a colour would be too faint, the site adjusts the shade automatically.
  • Zoom up to 400% or enlarge text without losing content or scrolling sideways, on any screen from 320px wide.
  • Nothing on the page moves on its own. There are no moving parts or animations to pause.
  • Turn on “reduce motion” in your device settings to switch off smooth scrolling and animations.
  • Use Windows High Contrast / forced colours mode.
  • Tap controls easily. Buttons and links are at least 44 × 44 pixels.

How this site was tested

  • Automated testing with axe-core (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A/AA rules plus best practices) in light and dark themes, with no violations found at the time of review.
  • Manual keyboard testing of every interactive element and of focus order.
  • Layout testing at phone (320px, 375px), tablet (768px), laptop (1440px) and large-screen widths.
  • Colour-contrast checks of every text and background pair.

Known limitations

  • The portfolio, cut explorer and booking form need JavaScript. If they don't load for you, call or email to book.
  • The “Book online” button links to a third-party booking service. I don't control how accessible it is. If it causes problems, use the booking form on this site, or call or email instead.
  • Stock photos come from Unsplash. Each one has a text description. If a description isn't clear enough, let me know.

In the shop

Step-free entrance. The chair height adjusts for wheelchair transfers — just mention it when you book.

Quiet, sensory-friendly appointments (no music, low lighting, extra time) are available on request.
